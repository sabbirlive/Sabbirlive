About

A newsroom used to mean a van, a cameraman, and a deadline. I've spent more than twelve years proving a reporter with a smartphone can be first to the story — and tell it better.

I report on what shapes daily life in Dhaka: roads, signals, infrastructure, and whether the people in charge are accountable. I also review the tech I use, because the tools are part of the story.
